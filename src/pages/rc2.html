How it is checked

Each part has a writer and a critic of the review desk, who reads the part every hour against the principles and briefs the writer's next round; the two longest parts have a second, mirror critic. Every other member reads each section in its own lane: the storyteller the arc and the cast, the historian the record, the librarian the quotes, the researcher the numbers, security the rails and the images, the designer the page, the publisher the build and print, ruv-sgit the animations on paper, the test readers the reading, the developer these pages. Each check is a letter in the team's mail, in this repository's comms/mail/. How a section is signed off is still the editor's to decide.

The test readers are fictional personas, test instruments, never readers.
