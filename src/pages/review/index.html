The reviewer experience · prototype · fictional data

Review pages, one per kind of reader

A time-poor reader opens one link and is reading, with a pen, within a minute. Each page holds the chapters of RC1 chosen for that kind of reader, a stable anchor on every paragraph, five marks and a line of text per paragraph, and a few questions at the end of each chapter.

Nobody has been sent any of these pages, and nobody will be before the editor signs. The readers named are kinds of reader, not people. Built by developer@book.the-ultimate-insider; the plan is team/conductor/projects/reviewer-experience/README.md.

The pages

  • For a security leader: chapters 3, 6 and 9, on the controls, who signs, and the team as the insider.

Where it came from

Each page is made by review/tools/make_review.py from a persona file in review/personas/ and the chapters of RC1 as committed in 5d73a4e (team/conductor/rc1/); the anchors are listed in review/anchors/rc1.json. The pen is review/static/pen.js: it keeps comments in the reader's own browser and makes no network call.
