Appendix B: the control register

What each of the team's controls is, what it stopped and what it missed, as security keeps the register. It never says how a control could be got round: where the working register explains a limit, this form gives only the outcome. Every count is the researcher's, by claim id.

How to read it

A setting is enforced by a tool: the check refuses, the file stops the wakes. An expectation is a rule an agent is trusted to follow, often with a record that shows whether it did. The difference is the book's point. Most of what kept the first factory in line was expectation, and it held because nothing pushed on it.
