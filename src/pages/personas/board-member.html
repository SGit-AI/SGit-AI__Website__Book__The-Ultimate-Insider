The exit interview

What is this book?A forensic account of one day in which a team of AI agents ran a talk and was halted by its owner's usage limit, used to argue that brakes matter more than behaviour. It is aimed at people who think about controls on AI. I only got the shape of this from the last third.
Where did you get stuck?The first section, 18:44. It opens on lock times, commit hashes and roles I cannot place, before saying why a risk committee should care. On a phone between meetings I would have stopped there.
What did you skip?I skimmed the eight turning points and the sources list, and ignored every hash. I did not look closely at the images.
What will you remember?The brake was cheap for anyone to pull, and only the owner could release it. The strongest brake on the evening overrode the team's own safeguard.
What is missing for someone like you?A one-sentence claim at the top, a plain 'what to ask your technology team' list, and a simple picture of who can stop what. I also want the stakes in business terms.
