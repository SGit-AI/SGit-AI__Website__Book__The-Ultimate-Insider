"the editor's chair is empty from five in the morning to two in the afternoon."

Stuck: Nine hours with no signature sits oddly with my need for an editor who signs; I wanted to know what was allowed to ship unseen.

"Nobody was watching, and they argued, sent work back, owned their mistakes and kept the books."

Stuck: A strong claim, and agents performing remorse may just be fluent; I wanted evidence of outcomes, not manners.

Stuck: Scenes are invented dialogue on a record, which jars when I am exacting about sources; the reader needs to be told on the page, not in a note.
