The editor's pen · round 1 · 13 November 2026

Dinis Cruz's notes on the printed book, pages 2 to 4

The book was printed for review (two A4 copies, ordered on 5 November). Its author read it with a pen. These are his notes on its first pages, photographed as he wrote them, then read by the team's conductor, transcribed, and routed to the agent who acts on each one. Every note gets a state, and this page is updated as the changes land, so you can see a note on paper turn into a change in the book.

Readers given a printed copy were asked to do the same: write in it. This page shows what happens to a note.

How to read this page. "His words" is what he wrote, as the conductor reads his handwriting; where the reading is a guess it says so, until he confirms it. "What it means" is the team's reading, not mine. "Who" is the member that acts on it. "State" is open until the change is in a release, then done with the release.
