The steps, in order

  1. Create the book on Leanpub, choosing the writing mode where you upload your own PDF (Leanpub's screens name it; where this page and the screen disagree, the screen wins). The team's earlier plan was Leanpub building from the repository (L2), but Leanpub's own build would not have this PDF's layout: the opening spread, the visual index, the footer bars. Uploading the PDF keeps the book exactly as it prints.
  2. Upload the PDF below.
  3. Upload the cover: Settings, Book Cover, the 1800 by 2700 image below.
  4. Paste the book page: Settings, About, the texts below.
  5. Set the price: a $0 minimum, $19.99 suggested, and untick the membership box (below).
  6. Preview, then Publish.

3. The book page, ready to paste

From the publisher's listing (publishing/listing.md), traced by the researcher and cleared by security on 2 November. Every number in it rests on the register.

Title

One Human, Nine Agents, Nineteen Hours

Subtitle

How a factory of AI agents built a presentation in a day, and how to build yours

Teaser

One person, nine AI agents and one day: how a factory of agents built a talk, and how you can set up your own and start the same day.

Meta description (136 characters)

One person and nine AI agents built a talk in nineteen hours. How the factory worked, and how to set up your own, starting the same day.

Description

For anyone with an idea and no team yet.

On 29 October 2026, Dinis Cruz opened an empty project, wrote a brief and one page of rules, and asked a team of AI agents to build his next talk with him. Nine agents joined, each with one job written down. That evening, nineteen hours after the first commit, he gave the talk.

This book follows that day as a factory. Who did what, and when. The five steps the team ran on its turns, from the first night: plan the work, have the plan checked, do the work, have the work checked, check it went to plan. The one short file that anyone on the team could use to stop everything, and that only he could lift. And what did not work, because that is in the book too.

The scenes are dramatised and marked as such; the events are the record's. Every number in the book rests on a register you can check, and the team's record is open for you to read.

Then it is your turn. One agent, one page of rules in your own words, one brief, and one small, finished piece of work on the first day. A second agent only when the first is busy. Alone or with others, you can start the same day.

Part of the Gen ZX Founders series: books about you starting, and building.

One line to check before pasting: "The scenes are dramatised and marked as such". The word "dramatised" is your open note 6 on the printed copy; if you pick another word for the tag, use it here too (for example "The scenes are retold from the record and marked as such").
