The book is about the factory

On 29 October 2026 a creator was given a factory of AI agents: a team assembled in hours around one objective, a talk, and taken apart when the job was done. It gave him a feedback loop he never had with slides: see it, ask for it differently, see it again. The talk was the side effect. The book is how the factory was built, how it ran, how it was stopped by one rule, and how it was rebuilt to write this book. Read it, and set up your own.

The series

Gen ZX Founders is not a range of birth years. It is the people building because finally they can: the youngest and the most experienced side by side. Every book in the series is a working example you can copy, about you doing it.
