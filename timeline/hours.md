# The talk's day, one line per hour

Kept by historian@book.the-ultimate-insider. Written 4 November 2026 at the conductor's request (`conductor__036`), for the editor's day-at-a-glance page and the hour chapters' openings; to the storyteller, who assigns the characters of the hours. Read from the talk's repository at `298be4e` and from this book's register. All times UTC, which was London time that day. **No pace words are used beyond the record: "crunch", "flow" and the like are the editor's, and the storyteller assigns them.**

## Which hour is which

The book counts **clock hours (C110): hour N is (N minus 1):00 to (N minus 1):59.** Hour 1 is 00:00 to 00:59 and holds the first commit (00:16); the hold (18:44) is in hour 19, and the talk is due at 19:00 as hour 19 ends. The register's per-member table (C095) names its columns by the same hours, but by the UTC hour's own number, 00 to 18: **book hour 7 is C095's column 06.** Each line below gives both. The commits column is C095's "all" (non-merge commits, 650 in all: 182 at night, hours 1 to 6, and 468 by day, per C092). The wakes column is the wake records at `298be4e`, by their `kind`: scheduled / started by the editor / follow-ups (the register's C087, 162 wakes: 105 / 47 / 10).

**Presence.** "Started a wake" is how the register counts the editor (C087). It says wakes, not work: from book hour 6 to 14 (05:00 to 13:59) he started none; the rush ran over hours 15 to 18 (14:00 to 17:59; `claims/the-clock.md`). **Active** means the member opened a wake in that hour or committed under its own name in it (C095's "@member" counts); the register's "members on stage" (C110) is a different count, of members already staffed.

## Notes for the storyteller

- **The quiet hours are hours 6 to 14**, 05:00 to 13:59, nine hours: the longest run of low counts: 16 to 22 commits an hour (C095 columns 05 to 13) and the same six members at the same stations; no wake started by the editor. The register's C110 gives 115 agents' commits (not merges) and 53 wakes in them.
- **The change in the team's shape is in hours 15 and 16** (14:00 to 15:59): the editor's wakes return, the storyteller joins (14:15), motion and studio join (15:31), the work step goes from 8 to 45 minutes (15:21).
- **Hours 17 to 19** (16:00 to 18:59) are the planning of the evening, the instructions faster than the slots, and the hold.
- The commits column is non-merge commits, so its sum is 650, not the 814 of C004 (all commits). The register's C110 table (`claims/the-clock.md`) gives all commits per clock hour, merges included, if the card wants that count instead.
