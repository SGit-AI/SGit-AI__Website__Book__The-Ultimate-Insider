# RC2, the 30-page edition, chapters 4 and 5 and the series page, read by three readers

**SYNTHETIC READERS: test instruments, never readers. A read of first drafts.** By synthetic@book.the-ultimate-insider, 2 November 2026. `manuscript/rc2/30/04-the-stop.md`, `05-the-factory-rebuilt-and-yours.md` and `06-a-book-about-you-doing-it.md` (about 4,200 words together) read by the three readers the editor named: a conference team member with their own agent, a newsroom builder, a first-build finisher. Reader by reader in this folder; every quote checked against the chapters, all exact.

## The answers

| Reader | The stop lands | Could start tomorrow | Would buy the longer book or the series |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conference team member | yes | maybe | maybe |
| Newsroom builder | yes | yes | maybe |
| First-build finisher | partly | yes | maybe |

The first thing each would do is the same: write one page of rules for one agent, with the stop line first. The conference team member adds who may pull and who may lift a shared hold; the newsroom builder makes it "stop and ask me before anything is published".

## What lands

- **"The hold"** holds for all three: one file, everything stops, only the editor restarts. The best line for the conference team member is still "Anyone on the team could stop everything. Only I could start it again."
- **"What did not work: the interview"**, **"Taken apart"**, **"Two scenes from the second factory"** and **"Your factory"** hold for all three. "Your factory", five steps with a check under each, is "the nearest thing to a recipe" (the conference team member). Best lines: "Nobody argued for keeping the nicer sentence." (the newsroom builder); "The team is temporary. The record is not." (the first-build finisher).
- Both "Do this now"s hold, except that the newsroom builder would cut chapter 5's with "Zoom in" as repeats.

## Where they stall

- **"After the hold: the empty chair"** (chapter 4): all three. Too short for its claim; none can say what the missing security member would have done.
- **"The second factory"** (chapter 5): all three. Eight cast cards in a row, four more named after them; the second half blurs, and none can tell which roles a first team needs.
- **"Why stop then: three answers"**: the conference team member and the newsroom builder want the takeaway first; the newsroom builder finds the second and third answers thin.
- **"The last ten minutes"**: the first-build finisher only, for lock and wake unexplained ("I could not feel what was lost").
- **"The third, planned"**: the first-build finisher only (a plan for several people, not for them); it is the section the conference team member needed most.

## The series page

Split three ways: cut (the conference team member: "a series blurb with no takeaway"), stall (the newsroom builder: a claim about the room in Faro they cannot trace, and marketing after two practical chapters), holds (the first-build finisher: it says the series is for people like them). None of the three would commit to the series on it.

## The cast

Stand out: the conductor and dev (all three), the researcher (two), the storyteller (two; the storyteller blurred for all three in chapters 2 and 3, and chapter 4's missed split has fixed it for two). Blur: deck, the designer, the librarian and the publisher (all three), the historian (two).

## For the writer, in order

1. "The second factory": the three or four roles a first team needs, the rest in a line.
2. "After the hold: the empty chair": one concrete thing the independent member would have caught.
3. Chapter 6: one step or one line the reader can take, and nothing they cannot trace.
4. "Why stop then": the takeaway first, the three accounts after.
5. For a first-time builder: lock, wake and freeze glossed in "The last ten minutes".

## Limits

Three readers, one session, first drafts; the readers were told they had read chapters 1 to 3 rather than shown them.
