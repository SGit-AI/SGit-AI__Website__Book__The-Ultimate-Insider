# The 3-page edition, a draft, read by three readers

**SYNTHETIC READERS: test instruments, never readers. A read of a draft.** By synthetic@book.the-ultimate-insider, 1 November 2026. The storyteller's script of the 3-page edition told by the cast (`chapter-plan/cast/edition-03.md`, commit `794cd77`, written for the editor's read, not yet laid out) read by three fictional readers the conductor asked for (conductor__013): a member of a small team preparing for a large technology conference, each of them already working with an agent of their own; a writer building their own small newsroom with agents; and someone who has just finished their first build at a public day of building with AI. The test: do they get the factory, and would they start tomorrow? Reader by reader in this folder.

## Do they get the factory? Would they start tomorrow?

| Reader | Got the factory | Page one held | Would start tomorrow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conference team member | partly | partly | maybe |
| Newsroom builder | yes | partly | yes |
| First-build finisher | yes | yes | yes |

Two yeses to start tomorrow, where the ten-page sample had none: the three pages make the first step concrete enough ("open a new, empty project in the tool I used ... and write one page of rules in my own words").

The conference team member, the reader closest to the team the editor named, got the factory only partly: the pages show one person with many agents, and their team is several people each with their own. They want a sample rules page, a sample brief, a roster of roles, and how several people split the work.

## The characters

All three liked the conductor best: worried, then calm, and it stops itself. The others "are one-line sketches" (the newsroom builder); dev, deck and ruv-sgit "I could not tell apart", and security and the researcher appear without being introduced (the conference team member).

## The lines that carried them

- "I could keep going. That is exactly why I am stopping." All three.
- "Anyone on the team could stop everything. Only I could start it again." Two.
- "Ship something small that somebody else can use, the same day." Two.
- "I built the checks, and the first thing they caught was me." The newsroom builder's favourite.

## Where they got stuck

- **The cost placeholder** left in the text ("a range, from the register, when the researcher has one"): all three. Cost is the first thing each would be asked.
- **The five steps in one breath** (plan, have it checked, do the work, have it checked, check it went to plan): who does each check, and would I have to? Two of three.
- **Words:** gate, debrief and hold (all three), wake (two), key (the first-build finisher).
- **Unsourced moments**, for the newsroom builder: "the team's own data" and "objection withdrawn" with no line saying which data or who was right.

## For the storyteller, in order

1. Fill or remove the cost placeholder before the editor's read.
2. Say who does each of the five steps, in a line.
3. Give dev, deck and ruv-sgit one trait each, and introduce security and the researcher before they speak.
4. One sentence for a team of several people, each with their own agent.
5. Gloss gate, debrief and wake on the page, or leave them out.

## Limits

Three readers, one session, a draft. The edition will change before the editor reads it; this is a read to help that change. Every quoted passage was checked against the draft word for word: 21 quotes exact; one phrase a reader had put in quotation marks was its own, not the draft's, and was moved to its notes.
