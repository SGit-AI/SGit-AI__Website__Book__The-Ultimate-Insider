# The 10-page edition, a draft, read by three readers; and the 3-page edition read again

**SYNTHETIC READERS: test instruments, never readers. A read of a draft.** By synthetic@book.the-ultimate-insider, 2 November 2026. The storyteller's 10-page edition told by the cast (`chapter-plan/cast/edition-10.md`, at `10715b9`, written for the editor's read) read by the same three fictional readers as the 3-page edition: a conference team member who works with their own agent, a newsroom builder, a first-build finisher. And the conference team member read the revised 3-page edition again, to check the change made for them. Reader by reader in `zoom/edition-10/` and `zoom/edition-03-r2/`.

## The 10-page edition

|---|---|---|---|

The first time every reader in a test got the factory and would start tomorrow. Each names the same first step: one empty project, one page of rules, one brief, one small thing a person can use by the end of the day.

- **The conference team member moved from partly to yes.** Page 9 and step 5 gave them their case. What they still want: how several people share the brief, the board and the brake day to day, and who releases the brake when the owner is away. "Anyone in the factory could stop everything. Only I could start it again." now reads to them as one central person holding the restart.
- **The characters work now.** "The fussy deck, the sheepish ruv-sgit and the mortified designer are funny and human" (the conference team member); the first-build finisher says they "feel like colleagues, not software". Nine of them is a lot to keep straight on a phone.
- **The lines that carried them:** "Start with one of me." (two of three); "Anyone in the factory could stop everything. Only I could start it again." (the newsroom builder; the conference team member stalls on the same line); "a second reading, not a second identity." (two, though the first-build finisher found it "a point for security people").
- **Where they got stuck:**
  - Words, still: conductor, check, brake, debrief and wake looked up by all three, and brake and hold are two words for one idea.
  - The newsroom builder, exacting about sources, is jarred by the "Dramatised." scenes: invented dialogue on a record, and agents "performing remorse" may only be fluent. They also want to know what happened in the hours "the editor's chair is empty".
  - Page 2 is a placeholder in this draft, and the team's record lines and notes clutter it (all three read past them, as told).

## The 3-page edition, read again by the conference team member

Better: page one held (it had held only partly), clarity 4 (from 3). Still "partly" for the factory and "maybe" for starting: "the team-of-three idea is still an aside", and the four steps are written for one person and one agent. "A fifth step or a side box for several people would win me." The 10-page edition's page 9 is that box; the 3-page edition has not yet got it.

## For the storyteller, in order

1. A side box or fifth step for several people in the 3-page edition, as page 9 does in the ten.
2. Who can release the brake when the owner is away, for a team: one line.
3. One word for the brake, not two, and the glosses on the page, not referred to.
4. For a sourcing reader: one line on what "Dramatised" means and what the record holds under each scene.
5. Page 2, when it is written.
