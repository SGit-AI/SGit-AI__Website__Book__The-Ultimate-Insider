# Round 1 of the editions, the readers' lane (ED1)

**SYNTHETIC READERS: test instruments, never readers.** By synthetic@book.the-ultimate-insider, 2 November 2026. The publisher's laid-out 3- and 10-page editions (`manuscript/editions/03` and `10` at `90bbcba`, built with `publishing/build_book.py`: 6 and 12 PDF pages) read as PDFs by the three readers the editor named: a conference team member with their own agent, a newsroom builder, a first-build finisher. Words and page together. Reader by reader in this folder, each with eight changes for the storyteller, designer or publisher.

## The verdicts

| Edition | Page one held | Got the factory | Would start tomorrow |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3-page | partly, partly, partly | yes, yes, yes | yes, yes, yes |
| 10-page | partly, partly, partly | yes, yes, yes | maybe, maybe, maybe |

**What changed from the drafts.** Read as scripts, the 10-page edition had three yeses to start tomorrow; laid out, three maybes. The words are almost the same; the page is what moved them. The action page, the one they all call the best, is page 11 of 12 ("I wade through eight pages of other people's arguments before I am told what to do"), and the pages before it read as unfinished: pictures described instead of drawn, and half-empty sheets.

## What all three say

- **Best page: the 10-page edition's page 11**, five numbered cards, one action each. "The only page that tells me what to do and it is easy to scan" (the first-build finisher).
- **Worst page: the contents page**, ten bare titles with no page numbers on an otherwise empty sheet; the same fault on the 3-page edition's page 2.
- **The pictures are not there.** Where a picture or a cast card belongs, the page shows its stage direction in italics ("One picture: the editor at a lectern ..."): "a script with holes".
- **Half-empty pages.** Most scene pages end with more than half the sheet blank; "the pages feel unfinished rather than airy".
- **The cover is a bare title.** All three want one line of promise: who it is for, and what they will have done by the end.

## What most say

- Two grey boxes (the words, and how to read the scenes) stand between the reader and the first story in the 3-page edition (two of three).
- Page 8's title "the therapy session" reads as a joke, not the hold; and the "three accounts" of the stop, the part a sourcing reader trusts most, sits in small type in a box (all three name page 8).
- "Where this came from" prints repository paths in mixed type (two of three).

## Not a finding

Two readers flagged a running header, "Round 1 of the editions, read by synthetic readers". That was my stamp for the copies I built for this read, not the publisher's; it is left out of the changes.

## The changes, by owner

- **Storyteller:** a promise line under the subtitle; the glosses after the first scene, or three of them; the action page or a pointer to it much earlier; page 8 retitled to say what happened, the three accounts in the body; the team case forward; name the claim the researcher checked.
- **Designer:** draw the pictures and cast cards the directions promise, or take the directions off the page (start with the factory's nine stations); fill the half-empty pages (a cast card, a clock strip); the 3-page edition's four steps as numbered cards like the ten's page 11.
- **Publisher:** page numbers on the contents, or fold it into the cover; plain source names in "Where this came from", one typeface; the box and cast-bar type a point larger on a phone.

## Limits

Three readers, one session, round 1 of two. Their words are checked against the PDFs' text only where quoted from the editions; the readers also describe what they saw on the page, which a text check cannot confirm.
