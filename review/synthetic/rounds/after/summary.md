# After the two rounds: the editions as the editor's readers would read them

**SYNTHETIC READERS: test instruments, never readers.** By synthetic@book.the-ultimate-insider, 2 November 2026, for ED1's after-round checks. The editions as built after round 2 (`manuscript/editions/` at `af78a0c`, with cover A, the on-stage markers and the speakers' heads; 6 and 12 PDF pages, built with the publisher's exact command) read fresh, with no earlier reads given, by five fictional readers: the three the editor named (a conference team member with their own agent, a newsroom builder, a first-build finisher) and the Gen Z and Gen X founders of the series. A few pictures are still to come. Reader by reader in this folder.

**Every reader would start tomorrow from both editions, and every reader would share them.** Three would buy the longer book. On RC1 every persona that read it said "maybe"; these are the first yeses, on different pages and partly different readers, so a direction and not a measurement.

All five would read the 3-page edition first ("it gets to the four steps by page 5"). The page they would send a friend is the steps page: page 5 of the 3-page edition or page 11 of the 10-page one.

## What each would do tomorrow

The same first step in five lives: an empty project, one page of rules for one agent (what it is for, what it may change, when it stops and asks), and a small first job: answering a bakery's customer questions, a researcher role for a newsletter, a helper for an app, a client-questions helper, a shared brief and board for a conference team.

## What is still wrong

- **The "Figure to come" boxes** (all five): on page 3 of the 10-page edition they are the first thing the content shows, and "the loudest unfinished thing in the book". The cover, markers and heads are in; the pictures that remain are the last thing between the editions and finished.
- **Page 9 of the 10-page edition splits the readers.** The newsroom builder calls it the best page ("the first place the book earns trust by naming a failure and its cost"); the conference team member and the first-build finisher call it the worst, because "freeze" and "the register" go unexplained under a dashed box. One plain sentence for each word would keep the first and win the others.
- **"Where this came from" as file paths** (the Gen Z and Gen X founders, the newsroom builder): "exactly the repo-speak that loses me".

## What the longer book would need, in their words

- A do-this-now and a worked example at every step, from a case like theirs (the first-build finisher, the Gen Z founder, the conference team member).
- A cost: "a real cost line for a student budget" (the Gen Z founder); "a real cost and hours account of a working team (even an estimate, plainly marked)" (the Gen X founder).
- Who answers when it goes wrong: the editor's signature as an actual gate, and what the reviewer does not protect against (the newsroom builder, the Gen X founder).
- For teams: three people, three agents, one brief, one board, and who holds the hold (the conference team member).

## Limits

Five readers, one session; the counts are not a measurement. The readers saw the editions as built today with some pictures to come; when the strips and drawings are placed, the "Figure to come" finding should go.
