## Two failures, two rules

**Narrator.** Two failures in this hour stay in. Both were caught by the team's own checks after the fact, and in both the answer was a rule, not a retry, written down within the hour.

**Narrator.** The first was the researcher's. In a wake I had asked for, its review after the work returned a finding: pages it had captured as evidence for the talk's claims had carried other sites' data in with them, and gone out with its first push. The researcher redacted it in the files and told me.

**Researcher**: My own review found it. After the push. A review that runs after the push is a review that runs late.

**Narrator.** That is the lesson the register draws: redaction does not reach a commit already pushed. The review worked; it worked at the wrong end. Hour five, below, shows what changed.

## Something to try

*A release that says it went out is not the same as a release that landed.*

*If your agents release on their own, try checking where "done" is measured: on the shared branch, or in the command's own report.*
