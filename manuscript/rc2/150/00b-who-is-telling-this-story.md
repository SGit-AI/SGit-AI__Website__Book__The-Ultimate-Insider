## It was an evolution

**Narrator.** This was not the first time I tried to make a talk behave differently. For the Black Hat Europe keynote of 9 December 2025, the slides became data: one file that the presentation read when it opened, so the slides could be changed like code. The VoiceDebrief pitch added the presenter: timings per slide, a countdown, my notes. And for a short demo for ruv.sgit.ai, due on 15 October, the whole presentation became a timeline of beats, with me speaking to it. Each earlier experiment fixed one thing and showed me the next. The day in this book is where they came together, with a team behind them for the first time.

## A book made the same way

**Narrator.** This book is being made the same way the talk was. A team of agents is writing it, drawing it and checking it: a storyteller who outlines each chapter, a researcher who keeps a register in which every number has an id, a historian who reconstructs the day from the record, a designer who drew the nine characters, and their four moods, in code, overnight. I think the way this book was made is as interesting as the day it describes, so you will see that too.

`%% record: HV1 (signed, brief 38: "can you add them all?"), printed: the newsroom's "I think the way this book was made is as interesting as the day it describes"`

`%% record: C122 (the book team's roles, as their own pages of rules state them; this book's record) C117 (the nine, four moods each, drawn by a script overnight, about three and three-quarter hours; this book's record)`

I wanted this to be a creative book, not only a correct one. If I stand up and say a talk was made by a team of agents and it looks like every talk before it, there is no reason to believe anything has changed. A book is the same. So this one has a cast, scenes, a clock in the margin and pictures that do the explaining. And it has me, saying what I think. Where the agents disagree with me, they say so too: at one point the researcher tells me a line of my own talk does not hold, and I keep the line, with the researcher's objection printed beside it.

This book has a companion website, at the-ultimate-insider.book.sgit.ai, where the team's pages and its record can be read as they stand.
