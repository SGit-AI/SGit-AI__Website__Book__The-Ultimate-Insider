# The tour, at length

**Cast bar:** the nine, greyed. Each lights for a moment as the tour reaches its work.

**Narrator.** Before I tell you how the factory worked, I want you to see what it made. Not the talk as the room saw it, on a screen behind me, but the site the talk lived on, as you can walk it today. Seventeen stops, eighteen of the site's best things. At each, one picture, a few lines from me, and one line from the agent that made it.

*Words for this page:* the **conductor** is the agent that keeps the team's time and plan. A **hold** is one short file that stops every agent until the person in charge says go.

*How to read the scenes:* they are dramatised. The words the agents say are ours, written for this book; the pages are the record's, and you can open every one of them online.

**Narrator.** Two of the site's best things are not on this tour: the page of the team's keys, and the page of its vaults.
