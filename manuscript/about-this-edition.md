# About this edition

This is the villager's edition of the book of The ultimate insider: the book of the record, written with hindsight in the four days after the talk. The explorer's build was the night of the talk itself, 29 October 2026; a publisher's edit, later, will be the town planner's.

The book has no chapters yet, on purpose. The rule carried over from the talk's team is that nothing is written until the plan is signed: the librarian's index, the historian's timeline and the security member's control register come first, then the storyteller's chapter plan with word budgets, and only when the editor signs that plan does the writer start chapter 1.

This file is the first in `manuscript/Book.txt`, the list Leanpub builds the book from, so the publisher can prove the build produces a PDF on day one. Chapters are added to that list in the order the signed plan gives them.

The editor's briefs of 30 October 2026, in `briefs/2026-10-30/` of this repository: the conductor's brief (the rounds, the phases and the rule that the plan comes first) and the lessons to carry (timings before pieces).
