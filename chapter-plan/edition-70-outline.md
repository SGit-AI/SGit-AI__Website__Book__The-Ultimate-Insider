## How the seventy grows from the thirty

The seventy keeps the five beats in the same order, so a reader who zooms in recognises where they are. Each of the thirty's chapters becomes a part of about fourteen pages, and the second pass of the study (`study-name-and-structure.md`: the tour, start with one agent, grow the team, the plumbing, making the talk then containing it, your turn) is folded into the beats where it belongs, rather than standing as six parts of its own. What the seventy adds is depth, not missing setup (the writer's question b): the plumbing explained, the containment chapter, the principles, and more text per agent with many pictures (the editor, 15:25 UTC, brief 09).
