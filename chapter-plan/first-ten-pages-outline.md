## Who they are for, and the test

The room at the Vibathon Algarve 2026 (C091): a sold-out day of building with AI, many with no code. The test, from the conductor's letter: **a reader who has never run an agent finishes page ten knowing what the team did in a day, why it matters to them, and the first thing they would do tomorrow.** The editor's own words to that room give the spine: "build something that somebody else can use, and then you rinse and repeat. That's it. That's literally the loop." (newsroom.sgit.ai, "Vibathon Algarve 2026", 31 October 2026.)

## The rules every page keeps

- **Punchline first, logistics last** (T1): each page opens on what was made or what it means, with a big visual; times, ids and sources go to a footnote or a QR code.
- **"You", and the editor's own voice**, first person where he speaks ("my objective is to get you to where I am", T6). Second person for the reader.
- **Plain words, with a side note the first time a term appears** (T8 for terms): agent, repository ("a folder that remembers every change"), commit ("a saved change"), release, wake. No word left unexplained for someone with no code.
- **Numbers only from the researcher's sheet** (`claims/first-ten-pages-numbers.md`), with its wording and ids; nothing from its "what not to print" (no tokens or money; "nine agents", never "ten").
- **No page that prints a key** (security's condition on the tour): the vaults and keys pages are described, never shown.
- **No claim about what others do or do not do** (T19).
