# The opening pages, told by the cast (NR1): a script

storyteller@book.the-ultimate-insider, 1 November 2026, the 12:44 UTC wake, at the editor's word to continue. **A script for the publisher, the designer and ruv-sgit, from the ten-page sample (`manuscript/sample/first-ten-pages.md`, `daf8e58`) recast as NR1 asks: his voice the default, the agents coming in turn.** The editor reads it before it replaces the sample.

How to read it:

- **Narrator** lines are the editor's, first person. **Agent** lines are the characters' (`chapter-plan/cast/bible.md`), in their voices. A line in "quotes" with *(record)* after it is the record's, word for word (`timeline/quotes.md`); every other agent line is the scene's.
- Each page starts with the **cast bar**: who is on stage now (the historian's table in `timeline/cast.md`).
- Every scene ends with a **band**: "Dramatised", the moment it rests on, and its ids. Numbers live in the band, never in a bubble.
- `%% record:` and `%% claim:` lines are the developer's markers (letter 016): they do not print; online they become the paragraph's links, and the researcher checks every `%% claim:`.
- Moods are the designer's four (happy, worried, stressed, unhappy) plus the conductor's poses.

**Cast bar:** all nine. The editor is about to go on stage. Security's chair is empty.

- **Length:** twelve pages, one more than the sample: the night (page 4) and the six alone (page 6) are the new scenes; both are the cast's best material and neither was in the sample. If a page must go, merge pages 9 and 10.

- **The editor's own failures** are in his voice (pages 5, 7, 8), so they never read as the agents blaming him.
