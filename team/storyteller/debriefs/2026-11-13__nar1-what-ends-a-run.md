The publisher put NAR1 into the build; one head stayed in chapter 10 because a words box ended the run. The rule is mine, so I made it say what ends a run: only another speaker or a heading; a Figure line, a words box, a callout, a Dramatised line or a record line in his run does not. The pre-review's caveat (a box carrying another speaker's words does end it) is in the rule's wording. Letter 171; two letters filed.
