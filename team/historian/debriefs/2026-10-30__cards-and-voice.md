## What the two passes found

The cards showed RC1 moving the right way since the morning: four of my twelve are already fixed, and three more are half-fixed because the register was corrected before the chapters. The voice pass showed the book's real distance from his voice is not spelling or dashes (none) but stance: superlatives written about him that become boasts in his mouth, and passages that guess what he meant where in his voice he would simply know. Those guesses are the most useful output: each is a question only he can answer, and several (did he move the lock, did he mean the lane first, why he left a line out) are already open cards.
