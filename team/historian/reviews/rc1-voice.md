## Method

The rules are the newsroom writer's, as the request gives them: first person; honest about where he stands; the memo's order; plain words; public sources named and dated; private people never named; no em-dashes; British spelling; no superlative self-claims; never naming the model that wrote it. ## What is already right

No em-dashes anywhere. British spelling throughout (one American spelling, inside a verbatim quote of his speech, `00b:77`, is his call). No private person named. No model named in prose; the file name `CLAUDE.md` appears as a path in chapters 2, 3, 4, 9, 11 and 12.

## The pattern

1. **Third person, everywhere.** Every chapter speaks of "the editor" or "the speaker": a global change, not listed line by line. It is heaviest in chapters 11b, 10, 6 and 13.
2. **Superlatives that become self-praise in his mouth.** "The most important", "the clearest", "extraordinary", "granted well", "the right outcome". Written about him they are judgements; in his voice they are boasts. Most rows below are this.
3. **Speaking for him.** Passages that say what he felt, meant or would have done ("by instinct", "the likelier reading", "for him, close to a product"). In his voice he knows the answer: these become questions to him, or his own words.
4. **Sources described, not named.** "His September article", "a widely cited breach report", "(dossier: ...)". His rule is a public source named and dated in the sentence.
5. **The argument before the events.** Chapters 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8 open on their conclusion; the memo's order is what happened, then what it means.

## Two decisions only he can make

- **A source whose title names the model.** His guide of 9 October 2026 (chapter 11b:51, :148, :150, :174) has the model in its title. "Name the public source" and "never name the model" cannot both hold: give the title, or describe and date it.
- **The commit author.** Appendix A's commands filter `--author=` with the model's name (`99:91`, `:93`), because that is the agents' commit author. Keep it as a command a reader can run, or filter by exclusion.

- `manuscript/13-concepts-and-principles.md:107` says the register "caught every claim that did not hold before it reached a slide (C030)"; Appendix A says none reached the stage, which is not the same. For the researcher.
