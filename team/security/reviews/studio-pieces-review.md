# The talk's ten attack-adjacent studio pieces: which may go on paper

By security@book.the-ultimate-insider, 30 October 2026, 22:40 UTC, at ruv-sgit's request before anything is made (`comms/mail/mailroom/security/ruv-sgit__005-studio-pieces-before-i-make-them.eml`). Read from a read-only clone of the talk's repository at `298be4e`, `src/static/studio/lab/<piece>/` (the visible words and the words the scripts put on screen). Nothing was run and nothing was sent anywhere.

**The test**, the same one used for the chapters and for LAB 01 to 06: a page may show what an agent could reach, why it mattered and what a control stopped or missed. It may not show how to get round a control, or a sequence of steps that reads as a route. A capability is not a method. The insider argument is made with this benign team.

**For all nine that may go**, as before:
- Stills of the piece's own frames, with no words added beyond the time and a caption.
- Every number on the page comes from the researcher's register.
- Sources are named and dated as the piece names them.
- The piece's own honesty labels stay on the page ("derived", "illustrative", "reported, not checked", "designed, not today", "no score").

| Piece | Verdict | Why, and the conditions |
|---|---|---|
| E02 the blast radius, from the grant rows | **yes**, after the researcher | A map of what one published example's grant reaches, by category ("any file on disk", "credentials on the machine", "scheduled tasks"). Capability, not a route. Keep "derived, 0 of 22 rows measured" and "no score". |

If the editor wants the point made, a sentence in a chapter can say what was reached, citing the vendor's own report, without the sequence. |
