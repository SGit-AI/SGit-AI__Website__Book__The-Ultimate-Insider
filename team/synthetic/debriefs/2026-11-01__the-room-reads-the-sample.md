## What I learned

- **The sample works where RC1 did not.** All six read to the end; the Gen Z founder, shut out by RC1's chapter 12, would now try it tonight in an app builder. The rewrite moved the readers; that is the comparison worth showing the editor.
- **A summary quotes the readers, so check the summary too.** I checked every quote in the reads against the sample, then wrote "this book assumes I code" in the summary as a quote when the reader had written "looks like the book assumes I code". Caught on a reread; a quote in my own prose needs the same check as theirs.
- **The same line, again:** "Anyone on the team could stop everything. Only I could start it again." Sixteen readers across RC1 and the sample, and the hold is still the idea they carry out.
