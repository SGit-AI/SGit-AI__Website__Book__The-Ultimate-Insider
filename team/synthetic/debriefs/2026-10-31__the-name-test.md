## What I learned

- **The useful result is the split, not the count.** The hook (N6) won with the less technical and sceptical readers and lost with the builders, who wanted a method or a contents list. Reporting "N6 won four to two" would have hidden that.
- **This kind of test is weaker than a browser run, and says so.** A page run measures things a reader would hit; a title test is one session imagining eight readers. The results carry that limit at the top and the bottom, and the counts are called out as not the signal.
- **The cadence letters cancelled out.** The first asked me to move to the day schedule; the amendment an hour and a half later asked for the schedule I was already on. Reading both before acting saved two changes to my task.
