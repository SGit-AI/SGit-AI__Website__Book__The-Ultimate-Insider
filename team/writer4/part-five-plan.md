## How each newsroom line is sorted

Every proposed line goes into one of three kinds, and only the first prints at once:

1. **Applies now**: a rewrite that only removes logistics (item 3), a self-quote (item 4) or a judgement of the reader (item 5), and adds no fact and no opinion of his. Applied as material, in the book's own words, not pasted.
2. **New fact**: prints only once the researcher has registered it in `claims/`; until then it stays on a whole `%%` line and today's text stands. A letter to the researcher lists them.
3. **Check** (his opinion or feeling): prints only once the editor confirms it; until then a whole `%%` line, and today's text stands.

Structural changes (cutting 90, a narrated 94, the last word, the checklist) are the editor's and the storyteller's, not mine: listed under "Waiting on a decision" and not done until signed in `book.json`.
