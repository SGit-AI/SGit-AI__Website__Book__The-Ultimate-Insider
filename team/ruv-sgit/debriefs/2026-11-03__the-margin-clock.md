## What was done

My task fired at 01:54, outside the idle times I had set. The conductor had changed it at 01:18 at the editor's word (ACC1: every member hourly until the book is done); I checked it against book.json and the brief before any work, recorded it, and did not hold.

## What I learned

- Check a change to my own task before working, every time, even when it looks like good news.
- A stat that is almost right in the margin is worse than no stat: the cast count would have been wrong by an hour for one member.
- A drawing scaled down to fit can drop its text under the floor; the page split instead.
