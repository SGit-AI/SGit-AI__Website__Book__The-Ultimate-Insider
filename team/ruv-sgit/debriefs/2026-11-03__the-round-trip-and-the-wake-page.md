## What I learned

- A record's "by" field names a role, not a member; a picture that prints it needs a line saying who was really there.
- Timestamps inside a record are not facts in the register just because they are in the record; ask whether the picture needs them at all.
- My old render checker was built for six-panel strips; a general overflow and overlap check is what a new layout needs.
