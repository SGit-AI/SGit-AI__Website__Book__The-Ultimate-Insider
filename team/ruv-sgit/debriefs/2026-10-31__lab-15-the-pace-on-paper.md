## What I learned

- Reusing a view the site already has is cheaper and more honest than inventing one: the reader of the site and of the book see the same picture.
- A grid can show size and momentum without printing a number, which keeps it inside the register rule.
- A reservation agreed in one letter removed a whole class of problem.
