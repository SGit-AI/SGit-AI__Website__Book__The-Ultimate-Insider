## How this pass applies the guidance

- **Brief 07, no logistics.** Times leave the narrator's mouth unless the time is the story ("before dawn", "four in the morning", "within three minutes"). The exact stamps stay in the bands and the record notes.
- **No quoting him to justify the text.** The narrator says the point; nothing here quotes Dinis at length.
- **The historian stops being the narrator's authority.** Where the text says "the historian reads it as", he reads it himself, and the record agrees.
- **Rule 11.** Lines that tell the reader how to read ("read that again", "read it the right way round", "so read what follows carefully") are rewritten as statements.
- **Connecting the dots, lightly.** The newsroom's agent team (named in the brief) as the source of the first rules; his security work behind "never instructions" and "a second reading, not a second identity"; the newsroom's habit of scanning before every commit, against the researcher's findings after the push.
- **Lines not listed here are fine as they are.** The narrator is already close to his voice in places in part 2: "I am not going to tell you either: it does not matter", "Small failures with small rules are the ones most teams never write down", "quiet is the evidence".
