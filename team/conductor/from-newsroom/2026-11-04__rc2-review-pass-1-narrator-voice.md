## 4. Whole-book patterns

- **Rhythm.** The clipped paired-sentence tic is mostly gone from the narrator. A few remain and are fine in moderation (p28, p62, p101). No action.
- **Logistics.** Clock stamps are now almost entirely in figures, cards and margins, which is right. In the narrator: p52 ("04:03"). "(record)" after quotes: p37, p43, p66, p74, p102.
- **Quoting him.** p43 (the freeze). The talk passages on p9 and p10 now say "As I said in the talk", which is the form brief 07 allowed.
- **Deferring to the historian.** Down to p34 (the interviews' preface) and p45 ("the lesson the register draws"). Elsewhere the historian appears as a character, which is right.
- **Rule 11.** No "read that again", "easy to miss", "obviously" or "underrated" found in 144 pages. Done.
- **Wording.** No em dashes. "Perfect" appears only in the talk's own slide title ("the perfect storm", p73, p117, p118), which is a quotation and stays, and once in the narrator, p39 ("did not go perfectly"), rewritten above. Superlatives in narrator lines: p10 (his quoted view), p103 and p121, handled above.
- **British English.** Consistent.
