## Structural notes before the lines

1. **Chapter 23's checklist repeats every "Do this now" word for word.** Brief 17 asks for no repeated content, and brief 12 turns the cards into invitations ("Something to try", in the separate file). So the checklist, as a list of commands, has no place in the printed first pass. Two options: (a) drop it, and keep only the figure, the one-page sheet with a box per chapter, carrying the new card titles; or (b) keep the sheet as the last page of the book, a thing to tear out. I would take (b), as a figure, with a one-line narrator introduction (proposed below).
2. **Chapter 23's five steps repeat Start here.** Brief 03 turns Start here into an overview and index, with the five steps "explained as a formula, not a to-do list". Then chapter 23 is where the steps are written out in full, once, with their "rests on" and "the moment", and Start here points to it. The two pages should not both carry the list.
3. **"Rests on" lines quote clock stamps** ("dev's first commit, at 00:40", "the check stopping dev's own release at 01:19", "the hold at 18:44"). By brief 07 these become "dev's first commit", "the check stopping dev's own release in the second hour", "the hold, a quarter of an hour before the talk". The chapter numbers stay.
4. **The second factory is staffed in two places.** Chapter 21's roll call and the back matter's "making of" both list the book's team joining. Proposed: the roll call stays in chapter 21 (it is a scene), and the making-of becomes a narrated page that points to it (see the back matter).
