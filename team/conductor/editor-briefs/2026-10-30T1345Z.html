The reviewer experience in one paragraph

One link per reviewer, theirs alone: the personal part travels in the URL's fragment, which never reaches a server. It opens a page that knows why we asked them, what to read (three chapters, about forty minutes) and the questions. Every paragraph takes a pen mark; comments go to you encrypted. When a revision ships, they see what their comments changed. Beside each step, one line on how the technology works, which is the showcase. First built with fictional data and tested by eight synthetic readers; then you; then one friendly reviewer; then the first wave.
