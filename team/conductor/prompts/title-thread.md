I want to nail down the title (and subtitle) of a book I am finishing, and I want to think it through with you interactively. Before we talk, please read the material below and come back with a short briefing, then we discuss.

**Update, 1 November 2026, from me:** the book is not about the presentation at all; the talk is a side effect, as a book about how a music festival was set up is not about the bands. It is about the factory: a team of agents assembled fast around a creator and one objective, then taken apart, built on earlier experiments; it gave me a huge feedback loop and control of the medium. Ideas: inventing on principle, the factory of agents, Wardley maps' commoditisation, Kevin Kelly's evolution of ideas. Readers: the Web Summit team I am assembling, people building their own newsroom, the Vibathon's finishers. The book also comes in editions of 1, 3, 10, 30, 70 and 150 pages (`book.json` PS1 and ZM1; `briefs/2026-11-01__in-session/03-the-factory-and-the-zoom.md`). Weigh every title against this.

**What the book is.** On 29 October 2026 a team of AI agents and I built a conference talk, "The ultimate insider", in a day, in the open: every brief, decision, commit and wake is public. A second team of agents is now writing the book of that day, in my voice. After reading the first draft (RC1) I decided the book is not about the insider argument (that is a separate book). Its centre of gravity is a pragmatic, example-driven walkthrough of agentic workflows as they run in October 2026: a multidisciplinary team of agents kept aligned by plain rules, shared protocols and open information, with provenance, determinism, memory and a human who signs. A reader should finish it able to do it themselves, because everything is open. The title should carry "agent", "agentic" or "agentic workflows" and say at a glance why someone would buy it.

**Decided already:** the book is part of a series, Gen ZX Founders (my book *Generation ZX Founders (GenAI Edition)* is the first): the series appears on the title page and the cover, and an early page explains the series and its philosophy: a book about you doing it, you starting, building what I built. The first ten pages are written for people like the room at today's Vibathon Algarve 2026 (students, people with an idea and no code, founders), who should finish them wanting to start.

**Still open (decision NS1):** the name and the structure. The team's current candidates: N7 *Agents That Work Together: a guided tour of agentic workflows, and how to build yours* (the team's recommendation), N8 *Brakes So We Can Go Faster: a guided tour of an agentic team at work, and how to build yours*, N2 *The Agentic Team*, N1 *Agentic Workflows, Shown Working*. The working title is still "The ultimate insider: the informed one". None of this is settled.
