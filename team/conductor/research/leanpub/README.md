## What they agree on

- **The $0 minimum fits Leanpub and its biggest books.** The three largest reader counts the studies could read (544,007, 195,514 and 136,067) are all free-minimum books, each sitting on an audience of its own (a course, a university, a non-profit); the fourth, at 115,385, is paid. The editor's own older books were all free-minimum, $7.99 to $11.00 suggested. Reader counts are not sales, and the free price is not shown to be the cause.
- **The slot is empty.** Nothing on Leanpub is titled "The Ultimate Insider", and no book treats the AI agent as the insider and shows the controls working on one recorded day. Nearest: "When AI Takes Action", "Zero Trust AI", "Harness Engineering", "Building Complex Software with GenAI".
- **Leanpub does not bring the readers; the audience engine does.** The talk, this repository and its site, and the interviews are ours; the book page should link to them and they to it.
- **A finished-looking page matters.** Every one of the editor's older pages shows a percentage under 100% and a last update of 2018 or earlier; that is what made them read as unfinished. This book should show it is complete and dated, and say each update plainly.

## The open question the pricing case rests on

The editor's correction (30 October 2026, about 12:15 UTC): he does not need to email readers directly; what matters is that every new version reaches them, and, above all, accessibility: charge for the content, but anyone who cannot afford it still has it. So the question below is only whether $0 readers receive new versions and their notices; a sign-up of his own is not needed.


Can the editor email readers who took the book at $0? What the studies found: Leanpub's "Notify your readers" on publishing a new version emails readers whose accounts accept new-version emails from the author (help article of 23 August 2023), for significant new content only, not offers; authors never get readers' email addresses (from a search summary, not read directly). Whether $0 readers receive these emails was not found. If they do not, or to keep a list of his own, the book's site needs its own sign-up. Sent to the publisher to settle.

## For the editor to decide (proposals, not decisions)

- The subtitle: a short title with a descriptive subtitle carrying the search words (study 3 suggests "How one person and a team of AI agents built a security talk in a day, and the controls that kept them in check").
- The categories: Artificial Intelligence, Computer Security, AI Governance, and Agentic or Harness Engineering (study 3).
- The suggested price: $19.99 now; security-leadership books suggest $29 to $35 (study 3); free-minimum bestsellers suggest $10 to $20 (study 4).
- Leanpub Pro for GitHub writing mode (about $18.99 a month on quarterly billing; one quarter is a bounded cost), or the free plan with pasted chapters (study 1).
- Paid promotion ($129 a week, $179 a month): only after a first signal, since newsletter readers of a $0 book mostly take it free (study 1). Not needed: the AI audiobook, translation, course and Amazon services.
