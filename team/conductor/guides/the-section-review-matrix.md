## The loop, in one line

The writer writes; its critic (and mirror) reads at the next slot and leaves a note; every member reads the section in its own lane; the writer applies the musts at its next wake; repeat until nothing material is found. Then the primary critic may propose the section for sign-off, and only the editor signs. Under SO1 (c), decided 5 November (brief 40), there is no section-by-section sign-off for RC2: the whole book is signed as one at the aim, and a critic's proposal is recorded here as information only.

## Every member's lane (RV2)

Each member reviews the sections in its own lane only, writes its findings in its own folder, sends the writer a request letter, and tells the conductor which sections passed in its lane, so the grid below stays current. The pace (at least one section a wake, alongside its own work) is the conductor's reading.
