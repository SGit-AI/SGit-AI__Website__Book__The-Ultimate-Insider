# The review programme: a personal request for comments

Started by the editor (Dinis Cruz) in the conductor's session on 30 October 2026, about 12:30 UTC. Kept by conductor@book.the-ultimate-insider. Nobody is contacted without the editor's go, each time.

## What it is for

Readers who review the book before and after it is on sale: sent a PDF (later a printed copy), and, because the book is built from source, a copy made for them: their kind of reader, the chapters that matter to them, and specific questions. In his words: "we could even send them a personalized version of that for review with comments ... To send that personalized PDF for them or for that type of reader, right? Let's say a security professional or a founder or this or that. So we can basically create a customized request for comments or review for them." And then follow it up.

## How it would work

1. **Reader types** (from the study in `team/conductor/research/reviewers/`): for each, the chapters, three to five questions, and what a good review gives the book.
2. **A review edition per type, or per person**, built like the companion: a one-page letter (who it is for, why them, what to read, the questions, by when), the chosen chapters with their page numbers kept, space for comments, and the book's record behind every number. The build is `team/conductor/rc1/tools/assemble_rc1.py` with a chapter list and a letter; the publisher owns the book's build, so the tool goes to the publisher as a request once the editor agrees.
3. **The short list**: public figures chosen for their public work, from the study. The editor picks who to ask; the conductor drafts the letter; the editor sends it, or says send.
4. **Follow-up**: each review request and its answer tracked in the repository without contact details (who by role, what was sent, the date, the verdict), and every quote used on a page only with the reviewer's permission.

## What waits on the editor

- Agree the programme, the reader types and the first wave (an open decision once the study is in).
- How a review copy is sent (Leanpub coupon, PDF, printed copy) and from which address (his own; nothing private goes in the repository).
