## Card by card

**01, changed.** The move happened: RC1 is in `manuscript/` on dev (18 files, with its 28 infographics, maps 6 to 16 and 50 call-outs as Markua since 17:30 UTC), and the two build fixes went in first. What the card still asks is yours alone: merge dev into main so Leanpub builds a first preview (L2). The options stand as drafted; A stays my recommendation, because only Leanpub's own preview tests the Markua that now carries the call-outs.

**02, changed.** `about-this-edition.md` was removed when RC1 moved in (`team/conductor/rc1/READY` allowed it), so the book opens with the release note and chapter 0, two notes, not three. The question that remains: does the release note become "About this edition" in the front matter when the first edition ships? My recommendation: yes, rewritten for the edition, unnumbered, with chapter 0 after it. The storyteller's draft 6 has chapter 0 as an open decision (D6-4); this card waits on that.

**03, adopted, with the syntax checked.** Markua does what option A needs: an id on the line above a heading (`{#id}`), a link to it (`[text](#id)`), and a "smart crosslink" that prints the chapter's number, `[chapter #n](#id)`. Chapter numbers are generated by Markua. One thing the spec does not settle: whether `#n` still prints a number when the headings are set without one (Markua's default heading format shows no number). That is a two-minute test on the first Leanpub preview, before any chapter is converted. Option C (titles in prose) stays the fallback.

**04, adopted.** Black ink for the Web Summit proof, every image checked in grey on the proof. The PDF and EPUB keep colour.

**05, adopted, with the sizes measured.** At the paperback's text width (about 4.7 inches) 300 dots per inch, the usual print guidance (KDP's page on it not re-read today), needs images about 1,400 pixels wide. The narrow ones are under that: `05-map-outside-inside` is 960 pixels wide (about 200 dots per inch in print), `09-wakes`, `12-dashboard` and `99-commits` 1,000. The three-slide strips are 1,600 by 448 pixels, which prints about 1.3 inches tall, too small for slide text. The proposal stands: strips re-made as one or two rows, maps from a text source at print resolution (the designer's and the conductor's pipelines, not mine).

**06, changed.** The card asked what asides are for; RC1 has since answered in practice: its 50 call-outs box concepts where they appear, numbers, warnings, take-aways and things to try, and the failures stay in the running prose. That is the card's option A plus three kinds. I recommend keeping it, and the boxes feeding a glossary (card 13). If you want it signed, it is A as RC1 built it.

**07, withdrawn.** Overtaken: the designer and the conductor's pipeline drew 28 infographics, now in the chapters.

**08, changed.** A designer is staffed and RC1 has a cover (`team/conductor/rc1/cover/cover-1.jpg`). What remains for the publisher: Leanpub wants 1,800 by 2,700 pixels and the paperback wrap needs the final page count (`publishing/cover_size.py`), so the wrap is made last. The choice of cover is yours and the designer's, not this card's.

**09, changed: the check is done, and it matters.** KDP's pricing page, which is part of the KDP agreement: Amazon may sell the ebook "at a lower price ... to match a third party's price", and "if your Digital Book is available through another sales channel for free, we may also make it available for free"; while matched to free, the royalty is zero. KDP's own list price cannot be set to free. So L1 (a $0 minimum on Leanpub) does not stop a Kindle listing, but Amazon may price the Kindle edition at free, and then it earns nothing. That sits with your reasons for L1 (readers first, access for those who cannot pay), and it is your choice whether it changes anything. Options as I would now put them: A: keep L1 and list on Kindle at the suggested price, accepting that Amazon may match free; B: keep L1 and do not list the ebook on Kindle, only the paperback; C: a paid floor on Leanpub when the Kindle edition goes on sale. I make no recommendation over your reasons for L1; A is consistent with them.

**10, adopted.** The ISBN is yours; the recommendation stands as drafted (your own if you expect another publisher's edition, KDP's free one otherwise).

**11, adopted.** A question for you, as drafted.

**12, adopted.** The sources stay at each chapter's end, set smaller.

**13, adopted.** Option B stays my recommendation: about the author and the licence on Leanpub day, colophon and glossary by Web Summit.

**14, changed.** Checked against Markua's spec: in `![text](path)` the bracket text is the image's alt text, and with no title given Markua shows the alt text as the caption (the default). So today every image's caption is also its alt text. To give a reader who cannot see the image something better than the caption, each image would get a title (the caption) and its own alt text: `![what the image shows](path "the caption")`. The EPUB still needs EPUBCheck (Java is in the container; EPUBCheck itself is not yet installed), and Appendix A's commands and chapter 10's table still need wrapping for small screens. Those are mine, next.
