How to read it

  1. The ring is one hour. Every member wakes once an hour, all day and all night.
  2. Each dot is a member, at its own minute. The conductor goes first, at :02, to route the hour's work; the storyteller last, at :54. No two share a minute.

  4. The counts are the talk's at 17:02 on 29 October, about two hours before the record ends: what the team had done by then, from its own records.
