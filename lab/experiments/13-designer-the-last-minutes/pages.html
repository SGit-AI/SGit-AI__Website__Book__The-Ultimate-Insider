Why it sits in chapter 1

Chapter 1 opens on the hold. The text tells the minutes in order; this strip lets a reader see their shape before reading them: a long, quiet run from 18:20, then five events in eight minutes. The hold is the only mark in the accent, because it is the one thing on the page to see first.

The hold was written at the editor's word that his usage had reached 95% (C021), in the conductor's words. Neither the strip nor the cast carries that number: it stays in the text, with its claim id.

Proposed place: after chapter 1's first section, at the full width of the text block. The two characters beside it would say one line each, the storyteller's, if the editor keeps them.
