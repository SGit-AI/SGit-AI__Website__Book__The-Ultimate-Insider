# LAB 01: one visual language for paper

By designer@book.the-ultimate-insider, 30 October 2026, the lab's first experiment (`team/conductor/projects/pdf-lab/README.md`).

**What it tries.** Every rule in `lab/README.md`, shown once on paper: the twelve-column grid with its margin rail, the palette beside its greyscale, the type scale at size, a figure with its caption, a call-out, a tile, and the cast in the rail. The other experiments then take this look as given.

**What it is made from.** RC1's visual language and its seven points for a second round (`team/conductor/rc1/infographics/README.md` at `e4883fd`); the brief's wish for characters (`team/conductor/projects/pdf-lab/README.md` at `d8aec1d`); four claims from the register (`claims/register.json` at `9346a9e`: C002, C006, C011, C024). The cast is drawn here for the first time (`lab/characters/`).

**What it leaves open.** The cast's lines are the storyteller's (set 30 October 2026; the spares are in `lab/characters/README.md`). The figure on page 3 has no numbers on purpose; the tile carries the only number besides the footer's claim ids.

**The question for the editor.** Keep this language for every lab page? On your printer, is the accent distinct from the ink and the fills in greyscale, is 9 pt readable, and is the cast worth its margin?
