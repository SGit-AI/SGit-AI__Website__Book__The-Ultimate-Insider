# LAB 27: the cast as personas

The editor's CH1 and NR1 (1 November 2026): the book told by its cast. A first proposal of the nine looks, four moods each, a scorecard each and the 'on stage now' marker.

- `python3 lab/characters/make_cast.py` draws the figures (`lab/characters/cast/<member>-<mood>.svg`, 20 x 25 mm).
- `python3 lab/experiments/27-designer-the-cast/make_sheets.py` lays out the four sheets, reading the scorecard values from `claims/scorecard-values.md` at build time.

Guard rails: AI agents only, the editor not drawn (only as himself, at his word); no names or commit ids in the drawings; every scorecard value from the register; a mood is the book's reading, never a number. The personalities are the storyteller's to write; the looks follow when they come.
