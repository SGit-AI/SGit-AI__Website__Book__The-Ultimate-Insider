What it shares, what it does not

It shares the Gen ZX Founders cover's system, read on gen-z-developers.sgit.ai/covers/ and copied from nowhere: a near-black ground, the title in stacked capitals that read at a thumbnail, a prompt line with a cursor.

It does not share its mark: the four bars here are this book's own colours and do not rise; they run into a short upright bar in the accent and stop, the book's idea of brakes that let you go faster.
