## What it tries

Chapter 9 reads the team's controls one by one in ten sections, then says what the misses had in common. This page puts the ten in one grid, grouped as the chapter sorts them (settings, enforced by a tool; expectations, trusted to the agent; and the one outside the repository), with what each stopped, what it missed, and the shape of the miss. Read down the last column and the chapter's argument is there: misses that reported without refusing, rested on the member running them, or waited on a role nobody held; the one brake pulled by a person; and the one control that refused on its own authority, C10, outside the repository.

## How it is made

`rows.json` holds each row in short phrases from the control's own section of `manuscript/09-the-team-was-the-insider.md` (read only), and names the section; the shapes are the chapter's own, from "What the controls missed, together". C7 and C9 are not in that summary, so their shapes are the chapter's words for them: "the channel" (C7) and "left open" (C9, from "the register leaves it open"). `tools/build.py` sets the grid in the lab's styles.

## What it keeps out
