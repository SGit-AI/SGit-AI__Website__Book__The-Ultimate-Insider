A picture paired with its text (pair.css): beside, and a words box with its mark

Words: a still slide is the picture you explain over; an animation is the picture that explains itself.

Sample words for the look only. A picture marked "%% layout: beside" sits on the right at about 55 percent of the measure, and its paragraphs run alongside it, so the page has no hole above or below it.

A picture marked "%% layout: full" crosses the measure and stays on the page of the paragraph that introduces it; a heading never waits alone at the foot of a page for the picture under it.

The words box keeps its fill and gains a small mark, an open book, so it reads as one of the page's visuals, as the boxes did in the version the editor liked.
