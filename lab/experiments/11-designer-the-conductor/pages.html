The conductor

A square robot with a clock for a face and a baton. It paces the work, keeps the plan, and is the first to notice the time. Its face is the clock, so its hands are its expression; the baton and the minute hand are its only accent.

Heada rounded square, 64 by 54, in the light fill
Facea clock: hour hand in ink, minute hand in the accent
Bodya smaller rounded square in the second fill
Batonheld in the right hand, its tip in the accent
Lineink, one weight; told apart from the cast by its square shape

pacing

Hands at ten past ten, baton up. The default.

One step at a time, on the clock. Small steps, every turn, add up to a book.

On the minute: plan it, check it, do it, check it again. Go.
