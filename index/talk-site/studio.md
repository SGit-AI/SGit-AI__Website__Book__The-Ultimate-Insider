## What it shows

- **B01, the perfect storm, has twelve lab versions** and three reviews (storyboard, v1, v2): the most worked piece of the talk, and the clearest case on the site of a piece made, judged and remade.
- **The SHarness storyline has ten lab pages**, a frame-by-frame story without and with the harness.
- **E01 to E14 each have a lab page**, and the reviewed ones a review page (`studio/reviews/e01.html` and on): the studio's method, version, review, keep or redo, is visible for every experiment.
- **The motion and port labs** hold the first version of each motion piece (M01 to M03) and of each port of ruv.sgit.ai's pieces, before they were set into the site.

For the book this is the one place where the talk's iteration can be shown rather than told: each review says what was kept and why. It is also the part of the site with the most pages a reader will never find from the menu.
