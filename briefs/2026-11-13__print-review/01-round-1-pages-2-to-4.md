## How the conductor reads it (not his words)

- **RS1, the restart**: the A4 copies printed well; the edition for sale stays Crown Quarto (KP1). The pause (PA1) ends at his word: every member's scheduled task is re-enabled and the loop runs again, with the editor's pen as its main input.
- **PEN1, the editor's pen**: each block of handwritten notes he sends becomes one public page (print-review-NN.html): the photographs, each note transcribed with uncertain readings marked, the team's reading, the owner and a state that the conductor updates as the changes land. Round 1 is pages 2 to 4, 30 notes (team/conductor/print-review/round-01.json). The Web Summit reader who took a copy on condition he writes in it will be sent the page.
- The handwritten-review process he remembers is not in this repository: this book's RC1 paper review (briefs/2026-10-31__rc1-paper-review/) was dictated, not photographed; the photographed process is likely the other book's.
